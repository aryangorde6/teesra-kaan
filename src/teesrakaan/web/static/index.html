      How it decides
      
              Muse Voice Transcribe writes down the call as it happens and keeps the two voices apart. After each thing the caller says, Muse Spark answers these questions about the caller, and must quote the caller's exact words for every "yes". A quote the caller never said is thrown away.
              
      
                
      
                Fixed rules raise the alarm, never the model on its own: Muse Spark must judge the call a scam, and at least one warning sign must be shown in the caller's own words. If one of them is a request for money, an OTP or PIN, an app or a link, it turns red.
                
    
                

    
                Demo calls are acted from scripts; they are not real. Nothing is recorded or stored.
